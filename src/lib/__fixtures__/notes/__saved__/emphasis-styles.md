# Emphasis

Bold as **underscores** and as **stars**. Italic as *stars* and as *underscores*. Both as ***stars*** and ***underscores***.

A snake_case_word and a file_name.md stay plain. Escapes: \*not italic\*, \_not italic\_, # not a heading, \[not a link\]. Prices like 5 \* 3 = 15 and a lone \* star.
